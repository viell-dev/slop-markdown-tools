# Tomatoes

> [!SUMMARY]
> Six plants in the south bed, started from seed in March and moved outside in May once the nights stayed above ten degrees.

They follow the [summer plan](<../Garden/Watering schedule.md#Summer>) and need *deep* watering rather than frequent watering.

| Variety | Planted | First fruit |
|---|---|---|
| Moneymaker | 4 May | 12 July |
| San Marzano | 11 May | 2 August |

The first fruit was picked on 12 July and the plants kept producing until the first frost, which is later than in any earlier year. ^harvest
